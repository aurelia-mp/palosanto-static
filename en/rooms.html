FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We answer the most common questions to help you plan every detail of your stay.

Accessibility

Q

Do you have accessible rooms for guests with reduced mobility?

A

Yes. We offer a Premium room specially designed for guests with reduced mobility. It is spacious, comfortable, and fully accessible.

To ensure availability, we recommend requesting it at the time of booking.

Families

Q

Do you offer connecting rooms for families or groups?

A

Yes. On the eighth floor, we have two Premium rooms connected through a private foyer, creating an exclusive and very comfortable space for families or groups of up to six people.

You may choose between King or Twin bedding, and add an extra bed in each room.

These rooms do not feature a balcony, but they compensate with open city views and direct access to the rooftop — one of Palo Santo’s most charming areas.

Q

Can extra beds be added?

A

Yes. All of our rooms can accommodate an extra bed for an additional charge.

When booking, simply select three guests to display the corresponding rate.

Q

I’m traveling with a baby — do you provide cribs?/p>

A

Of course. We can set up a foldaway crib suitable for babies up to two years old.

Please let us know in advance so we can have it ready upon arrival.

Amenities

Q

How many rooms does the hotel have?

A

The hotel has a total of 26 rooms.

Q

What is the difference between Premium and Deluxe rooms?

A

Deluxe rooms are located on higher floors and feature a more defined living area.

Q

Do the bathrooms have showers or bathtubs?

A

All bathrooms feature extra-large showers, except the Suites, which offer whirlpool bathtubs.

Q

Do the rooms include a kitchen?

A

No. Our rooms do not have kitchen facilities, but they do include an area with a minibar, Nespresso coffee machine (consumption not included), and electric kettle.

Q

Do all rooms have a private balcony?

A

Almost all of them.

The only exceptions are the two Premium rooms on the eighth floor, which do not have a balcony but offer beautiful panoramic views and immediate access to the rooftop.

A true boutique experience in Palermo.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about our rooms.

Accessibility

Do you have accessible rooms for guests with reduced mobility?

Yes. We have a Premium room specially designed for guests with reduced mobility: it is spacious, comfortable and fully accessible. To make sure it is available, we recommend requesting it when you book.

Families

Do you offer connecting rooms for families or groups?

Yes. On the eighth floor, two Premium rooms are connected through a private foyer, creating an exclusive and very comfortable space for families or groups of up to six people. You can choose King or Twin bedding and add an extra bed in each room.

These rooms do not have a balcony, but they make up for it with open city views and direct access to the rooftop, one of Palo Santo’s most charming spots.

Can extra beds be added?

Yes. Every room can take an extra bed, for an additional charge. When booking, select three guests to see the corresponding rate.

I’m traveling with a baby. Do you provide cribs?

Of course. We can set up a foldaway crib free of charge, suitable for babies up to two years old. Please let us know in advance so we can have it ready when you arrive.

Amenities

How many rooms does the hotel have?

The hotel has 26 rooms in total, across 8 floors.

What is the difference between Premium and Deluxe rooms?

Deluxe rooms are on the upper floors and have a more defined living area. All Deluxe rooms have a King bed, while Premium rooms can also be set up with two single beds.

Do the bathrooms have showers or bathtubs?

All bathrooms have an extra-large walk-in shower, except in the suites, which have a whirlpool bathtub.

Do the rooms have a kitchen?

No. Our rooms do not have a kitchen, but they do have an area with a minibar, a Nespresso coffee machine (consumption not included) and an electric kettle.

Do all rooms have a private balcony?

Almost all of them. The only exceptions are the two Premium rooms on the eighth floor, which have no balcony but offer beautiful panoramic views and immediate access to the rooftop.

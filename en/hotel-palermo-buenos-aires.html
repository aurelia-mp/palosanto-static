The neighborhood

Why Stay in Palermo Hollywood

For many travelers searching for a hotel in Palermo Buenos Aires, the choice of neighborhood is just as important as the hotel itself.

Cafes, restaurants, art galleries

Palermo Hollywood has become one of the most desirable areas to stay in the city. Originally known for its television and film studios, the neighborhood has evolved into a lively district filled with restaurants, cocktail bars, design stores and art spaces.

Experience Buenos Aires like a local

Staying in Palermo Hollywood allows you to experience Buenos Aires like a resident. The streets are lined with cafes, bakeries and small boutiques, and many of the city’s most celebrated restaurants are within walking distance. It offers a more local atmosphere than traditional tourist areas while remaining extremely convenient for visitors.

A well connected neighborhood

The neighborhood is also well connected to the rest of the city. From Palermo you can easily reach:

  • Recoleta, known for its museums and historic architecture
  • San Telmo, famous for its open-air street markets and antique shops
  • Downtown Buenos Aires, home to the Avenida 9 de Julio, Teatro Colón, and Plaza de Mayo

For travelers looking for a balance between culture, gastronomy and nightlife, Palermo Hollywood is often considered the best area to stay in Buenos Aires.

Sustainability

A Sustainable Boutique Hotel in the Heart of Palermo

Palo Santo Hotel is more than just a boutique hotel in Palermo. It was designed as a sustainable urban oasis that integrates architecture, nature and responsible hospitality.

The building incorporates vertical gardens that cover the facade and inner courtyards, helping to regulate temperature while bringing greenery into the city environment. These gardens also create a calm atmosphere that guests immediately notice upon arrival.

Sustainability is part of the hotel’s philosophy. The property was built following international environmental standards and includes features such as energy-efficient systems, water conservation strategies and environmentally responsible materials.

Inside the hotel, natural light and greenery are central elements of the design, creating a peaceful setting where guests can relax after a day exploring Buenos Aires.

This combination of design, sustainability and location has made Palo Santo one of the most distinctive boutique hotels in Palermo Hollywood.

Around the hotel

Restaurants and Attractions Near the Hotel

One of the advantages of staying in Palermo Hollywood is the extraordinary selection of restaurants nearby.

Within a few blocks of the hotel you will find some of Buenos Aires’ most celebrated dining spots. The area is known for its modern Argentine cuisine, innovative cocktail bars and vibrant food scene.

Guests frequently visit restaurants such as Don Julio, consistently ranked among the best steakhouses in the world, as well as many smaller neighborhood restaurants that showcase contemporary Argentine gastronomy.

Beyond restaurants, the neighborhood offers plenty to explore. A short walk from the hotel leads to boutiques and design stores in Palermo Soho. Numerous art galleries also reflect the creative spirit of Palermo.

Several cultural attractions are also easily accessible, including the MALBA museum, the parks of Palermo, the beautiful, tree-lined, Libertador Avenue, and the city’s botanical garden. These places make the neighborhood ideal for visitors who want to combine culture, outdoor spaces and gastronomy during their stay in Buenos Aires.

For live music lovers, Movistar Arena, one of the city’s main concert venues, is also located nearby, making it a convenient choice for those attending events there.

Location

Map and Location

Palo Santo Hotel is located in Palermo Hollywood, one of the most convenient neighborhoods for visitors to Buenos Aires.

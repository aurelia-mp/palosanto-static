Enjoy your own rooftop paradise: our one-bedroom Terrace Suite includes a large private terrace. With a separate bedroom and living room, this spacious suite features contemporary design, beautiful natural wood, and state-of-the art equipment.

A must-stay for honeymooners or a romantic getaway.

Detailed Room Information

Located on the sixth floor, the 55m2 / 590 sq. ft Terrace Suite features a private 215 square-foot/ 20m2 terrace.

The suite comprises one bedroom with an en-suite bathroom with a whirlpool tub, and a walk-in closet. The bedroom features a King-size double bed.

The living room is well appointed, with a dining area, and a sitting area. Two 55-inch Smart 4K TV will allow you to enjoy your streaming accounts or local cable channels both from the living room or the bedroom. Work comfortably from your desk or the dining table, with of course complimentary internet access. Next to the minibar, you will find an electric kettle, Nespresso coffee machine, cups, and glasses.

The beautiful 20 square meters (215 square feet) terrace is furnished with lounge chairs, a table and chairs, so that guests can relax and enjoy Palermo from a new perspective.

All of our rooms are smoke-free, including the balconies. For an additional cost, you can request an extra bed for a third adult or a child over three years old. We offer cribs free of charge (please confirm availability in advance) for babies and small children.

Boutique Experience

At Palo Santo, we are fully committed to help you make the most out of your stay in Buenos Aires.

We handpicked a list of local restaurants that we are sure you will enjoy. We personally redacted our Palo Santo city guide with our must-sees, architectural highlights and itineraries.

Our Front Desk staff is available 24hs a day, 7 days a week, to help you with all your requests: recommendations, reservations, transfers, etc.

Our rates always include:
  • Buffet Breakfast
  • WiFi
  • Complimentary bike (subject to availability)
  • Printed Palo Santo City Guide, available in Spanish and English
  • Access to our gym on the 7th floor, open from 9AM to 9PM
  • In the summer: access to our rooftop with a shallow pool and hot tub (outdoor area, not heated)

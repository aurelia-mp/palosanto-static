Large, light-filled rooms with a private balcony and view of the hotel’s vertical gardens.

Our Premium Rooms offer a fresh and laid-back atmosphere. Design furniture includes iconic pieces such as a Noguchi sofa, an Eames chair, a Charles-like bed and a Jacobsen chair. The bathroom is modern and features a walk-in shower and state-of-the-art equipment.

Detailed room information

Our 35 m2 / 377 ft2 Premium rooms feature one King bed or two Single beds. High-quality mattresses, thick blackout curtains, and double-glazed windows will ensure a good night’s sleep.

Rooms are equipped with a desk and free high-speed WiFi so you can work comfortably. The living area is fitted with a sofa, an armchair, and a coffee table. Next to the minibar, you will find an electric kettle, cups, and glasses. The 50-inch Smart 4K TV will allow you to access your streaming accounts or local cable channels.

The bathroom is spacious and comfortable. It features an extra-large walk-in shower, a hair dryer, bathrobes, and complete bathroom amenities.

The Premium rooms located from the 1st to the 3rd floor – four rooms per floor – have a private balcony, with city or garden views. The two Premium rooms on the 8th floor have direct access to the hotel rooftop and beautiful views of the city.

All of our rooms are smoke-free, including the balconies. For an additional cost, you can request an extra bed for a third adult or a child over three years old. We offer cribs free of charge (please confirm availability in advance) for babies and small children.

Boutique Experience

At Palo Santo, we are fully committed to help you make the most out of your stay in Buenos Aires.

We handpicked a list of local restaurants that we are sure you will enjoy. We personally redacted our Palo Santo city guide with our must-sees, architectural highlights and itineraries.

Our Front Desk staff is available 24hs a day, 7 days a week, to help you with all your requests: recommendations, reservations, transfers, etc.

Our rates always include:
  • Buffet Breakfast
  • WiFi
  • Complimentary bike (subject to availability)
  • Printed Palo Santo City Guide, available in Spanish and English
  • Access to our gym on the 7th floor, open from 9AM to 9PM
  • In the summer: access to our rooftop with a shallow pool and hot tub (outdoor area, not heated)

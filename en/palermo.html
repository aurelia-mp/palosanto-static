Getting around

Palermo Hollywood is well connected. The D subway line (Plaza Italia station) and the Metrobús are a few blocks away, and there are bike lanes right outside. But the best advice is usually the simplest: much of the neighborhood is best on foot.

The neighborhood

We chose Palermo for concrete reasons

Its sidewalks are wide and tree-shaded, the cafés open onto the street, and the dining scene is one of the most interesting in the region. For those looking for a boutique hotel in Palermo Hollywood, this part of the city is something specific: a place where local life happens at street level, and where the rest of Buenos Aires stays within easy reach.

To cover more ground, you can borrow one of our complimentary bicycles.

Everything you need, a short walk away

Dining

Acclaimed restaurants and specialty coffee roasters within a five-minute walk.

Design and art

Independent design stores, art galleries, and weekend markets.

Parks

Direct access to the parks, running paths, and the BA EcoBici bike-share network.

Neighborhood guide

Exploring the neighborhood

Palermo is many neighborhoods in one: Hollywood, where we are; the design of Soho; the stately calm of Chico; the nights of Las Cañitas. Each has its own character, and our blog covers them one by one, with where to eat, where to drink and what to see.

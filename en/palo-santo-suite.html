From the top floor, our one-bedroom Palo Santo Suite has beautiful views of the Palermo skyline. Two private balconies frame the hotel’s vertical gardens and display a unique perspective.

This elegant suite is composed of a large bedroom with a bathroom with whirlpool bath, and a living room with a dining area, sitting area, design furniture and a toilet. Here, everything is comfort and harmony: noble materials, clean, flawless design and contemporary furniture.

Detailed Room Information

Our one-bedroom Palo Santo Suite is the largest suite in the hotel. It comprises a living room and a bedroom, two balconies overlooking the city, a bathroom with a whirlpool bath, and a separate guest’s toilet.

The living room is generously sized and equipped with designer furniture. Relax in the large sitting area and enjoy your streaming accounts or local cable channels on the 55-inch Smart 4K TV. Work comfortably from your desk or the dining table, and enjoy complimentary high-speed WiFi. Next to the minibar, you will find an electric kettle, Nespresso coffee machine, cups, and glasses

The bedroom features a King-size double bed, another 55-inch Smart 4K TV, and a luxurious bathroom with a whirlpool tub.

All of our rooms are smoke-free, including the balconies. For an additional cost, you can request an extra bed for a third adult or a child over three years old. We offer cribs free of charge (please confirm availability in advance) for babies and small children.

Boutique Experience

At Palo Santo, we are fully committed to help you make the most out of your stay in Buenos Aires.

We handpicked a list of local restaurants that we are sure you will enjoy. We personally redacted our Palo Santo city guide with our must-sees, architectural highlights and itineraries.

Our Front Desk staff is available 24hs a day, 7 days a week, to help you with all your requests: recommendations, reservations, transfers, etc.

Our rates always include:
  • Buffet Breakfast
  • WiFi
  • Complimentary bike (subject to availability)
  • Printed Palo Santo City Guide, available in Spanish and English
  • Access to our gym on the 7th floor, open from 9AM to 9PM
  • In the summer: access to our rooftop with a shallow pool and hot tub (outdoor area, not heated)

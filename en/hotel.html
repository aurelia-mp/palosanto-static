Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions, to help you plan every detail of your stay.

Hotel policies

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 PM (noon).

My flight arrives early. Can I check in before 3:00 PM?

Early check-in depends on same-day availability. If you want to guarantee early access to your room, we recommend booking the previous night. Otherwise, you may leave your luggage at Reception and enjoy the city while we prepare your room.

I am arriving late at night. Will Reception be open?

Yes, our Reception is open 24 hours a day, every day.

Do you offer luggage storage?

Yes. We can store your luggage if you arrive before check-in time or if your flight leaves after check-out. We can also store one suitcase per person between two stays (space is limited).

Can I request a late check-out?

Yes. We offer late check-out until 6:00 PM, subject to availability and an additional fee. Please inquire at Reception the day prior or upon arrival.

Is smoking allowed at Palo Santo Hotel?

No. The hotel is completely smoke-free, including rooms, balconies, gardens and terraces. This is part of our sustainability and air-quality policy.

Do you accept pets?

We do not accept pets.

Services

Do you have parking?

We do not have on-site parking, but there are several private garages within 400 meters of the hotel.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. We can coordinate private transfers to and from the Buenos Aires airports. We only need your flight details.

Is the hotel accessible for guests with reduced mobility?

Yes. All common areas are accessible, with no steps on the ground floor and elevator access to all levels.

Does the hotel offer laundry service?

Yes, we offer laundry service with next-day delivery (depending on the time of request).

Breakfast

What are the breakfast hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, 7:30 AM to 11:00 AM. If you have an early flight, we can prepare a takeaway breakfast: please let us know in advance.

Do you offer options for special diets?

Yes. Our buffet includes vegetarian and vegan options. If you need gluten-free options, please tell us before your arrival so we can prepare them properly.

Buffet Breakfast

Breakfast

At Palo Santo, we care about sustainability from the very first bite of your day. Our buffet features local, seasonal and sustainable ingredients, served in our dining room with garden views.

Our buffet includes vegetarian and vegan options. If you follow a gluten-free diet, please let us know in advance so we can offer you adapted options.

Breakfast hours

  • Weekdays: 7am to 10:30am
  • Weekends and bank holidays: 7:30am to 11am

If you have an early flight, just let us know so we can prepare you a takeaway breakfast.

Buffet Breakfast menu

Restaurante Charqui

Restaurante Charqui

Charqui Restaurant specializes in tasty, hearty, homemade Argentine cuisine. Taste their slow-cooked ojo de bife (rib eye) with Andean potatoes or the braised pork with ñoquis and mushrooms.

Charqui’s weekend brunch is very popular as well with its offer of sweet and savory options: dulce de leche alfajor, fresh pastry, avocado toast, pulled-pork sandwich, etc.

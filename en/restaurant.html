Charqui restaurant

Argentine cooking, with garden views

Charqui specializes in tasty, hearty, homemade Argentine cuisine. Taste their slow-cooked ojo de bife (rib eye) with Andean potatoes or the braised pork with ñoquis and mushrooms.

Its weekend brunch is very popular too, with sweet and savory options: dulce de leche alfajor, fresh pastries, avocado toast and a pulled-pork sandwich.

Our rooms

Premium

35m2 / 377 sq.ft
1st to 3rd Floors + 8th Floor

Our most popular rooms: spacious, comfortable, and filled with natural light, our Premium Rooms (one King bed or two Single beds) are ideal for couples, friends, or families. All feature a private balcony, except for the two interconnecting Premium Rooms located on the 8th floor, perfect for guests traveling with children.

Deluxe

35m2 / 377 sq.ft
4h to 6th Floors

For exceptional comfort and quiet elegance. Located on the upper floors, our Deluxe Rooms are elegant, filled with natural light, and exceptionally comfortable. Designer furniture, a private balcony, an oversized shower, and a perfect King bed for true relaxation.

Terrace Suite

55m2 / 590 sq. ft
6th Floor

Our Terrace Suite, with its private terrace, is an oasis above the Buenos Aires skyline. A two-room suite offering contemporary luxury and exceptional comfort: beautiful natural woods, iconic design pieces, and state-of-the-art amenities.

Palo Santo Suite

65m2 / 700 sq. ft
7th Floor

The Palo Santo Suite is the hotel’s most generous space — a retreat designed for indulgence. Spread across 65 m², it features a King bed, separate living area, whirlpool bath, private balconies, and sweeping views of the Palermo skyline — an invitation to unwind and savor the city from above.

A unique green architecture

Palo Santo is the first urban green hotel in Argentina.
Built according to LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) standards, Palo Santo is a green hotel designed with sustainability in mind. Vertical gardens are a defining feature, supported by a comprehensive environmental program that addresses the many aspects of sustainable building.

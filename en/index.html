Before you travel

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions, to help you plan every detail of your stay.

See all FAQs
What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 PM (noon). Late check-out until 6:00 PM is available for an additional fee, subject to availability.

My flight arrives early — can I check in before 3:00 PM?

Early check-in depends on same-day availability. To guarantee early access to your room, we recommend booking the previous night. You can always leave your luggage at Reception.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. We can coordinate private transfers to and from Buenos Aires airports. We only need your flight details.

Do you have parking?

We do not have on-site parking, but there are several private garages within 400 meters of the hotel.

Do you accept pets?

We do not accept pets.

Sustainable construction

Modern luxury that cares for the environment

We believe that modern luxury must commit to preserving the environment. The hotel was built following LEED standards (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design), the highest sustainable construction standards in the United States.

Palo Santo’s operations are environmentally friendly too: we carefully select materials, equipment and suppliers to reduce the environmental impact of the hotel’s construction and operations.

Vertical gardens

Beauty with purpose

The facades, courtyards, gardens, balconies and terraces are all green. On the facades, specially designed iron structures guide the growth of two 28-meter vertical gardens, with over 900 plants in total.

Beyond their beauty, these plant curtains have an ecological purpose:

  • Natural insulation The plants insulate the building naturally.
  • They absorb CO₂ Ever-growing, they absorb our emissions.
  • Cleaner air They improve the city’s air quality.
  • A cooler microclimate They cool the air around the hotel.

Operations

01

Our day-to-day operations are designed to preserve resources and to recycle or reuse whenever possible.

  • We work with a cooperative for waste separation and recycling
  • We donate our used linens so they can be repurposed and reused
  • We provide complimentary bicycles to encourage sustainable mobility
  • We choose a certified, low-impact laundry service
  • Our cleaning products are low-impact
  • We invite you to reuse sheets and towels, if you wish
  • There is a refillable water bottle in every room
  • We limit printed materials and use recycled, unbleached paper
  • We choose eco-certified servers to host our website

Energy

02

Rational energy use is one of the pillars of our program. From the building’s construction, it guided the design of efficient rooms and the choice of equipment.

  • LED and energy-efficient lighting
  • Motion detectors to avoid unnecessary use
  • VRV Inverter air conditioning
  • High-performance insulation
  • Energy-efficient elevators
  • Key cards that switch off the power in the rooms

Water

03

At Palo Santo, we care for every drop.

  • We recover rainwater
  • We use efficient dual-flush toilets
  • We absorb rainwater to ease the load on the city’s storm drains
  • We use drip irrigation

Materials

04

We choose durable materials, so as not to waste resources.

  • FSC-certified wood
  • Marble and porcelain tiles, for their long life
  • Noble materials: iron, stone, concrete and wood
  • Natural cotton linens

Insulation

05

Besides providing acoustic comfort, maximizing insulation is a very efficient way to reduce energy use.

  • Double-glazed windows with an air chamber
  • Insulated, soundproof walls, with fiberglass and multiple layers
  • Plants insulate the roof, and climbing vines insulate the sides of the building

Air

06

We care for the hotel’s indoor air and reduce pollution.

  • The air in the rooms is filtered
  • Airflow in the rooms is measured to avoid unnecessary conditioning
  • Where possible, we use wallpaper instead of paint, to avoid polluting solvents

Carbon dioxide

07

We control our CO₂ emissions.

  • Over 900 ever-growing plants absorb CO₂
  • Ecological pruning, to avoid CO₂ emissions
  • Ozone-safe air conditioning equipment

Certifications and awards

A recognized commitment

LEED standards

We closely follow the green building standards defined by the US Green Building Council for LEED certification.

Hoteles Más Verdes award

In 2013 we received the Hoteles Más Verdes (Greenest Hotels) award for the best sustainable hotel project.
